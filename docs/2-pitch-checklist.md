# Pitch checklist for the big drama apps (our suggestion, not their rules)

From the AndyNoCode video about where to publish AI dramas and who pays. Round 2, 20 Nov 2026. No drama app publishes its own list of what to send, so this is our advice. We have not had a reply from any of them yet.

## Where to send it (door 1)

- **GoodShort:** e-mail copyright@goodnovel.com (its business page asks content providers to e-mail this address). Source: [goodshort.com/business](https://www.goodshort.com/business), our capture 19 Nov 2026.
- **ReelShort:** its studio's contact form, option "Story Ideas and Submissions". Source: [crazymaplestudio.com/contact](https://www.crazymaplestudio.com/contact), our capture 19 Nov 2026.
- **DramaBox:** the only public contact is dramaboxapp@gmail.com. Source: [support.dramaboxdb.com](https://support.dramaboxdb.com/), our capture 19 Nov 2026.

## Before you send

1. **Send a short pitch, not your whole script.** ReelShort's studio form makes you agree to a "Content Submission Agreement" that the page doesn't show.
2. **Finish a few episodes first.** These apps are built for professional studios. A pitch with finished episodes is easier to say yes to.
3. **Keep a copy** of everything you send, with the date.
4. **Never pay anyone to "get you in".** ReelShort's studio warns that anyone offering "investment returns" in ReelShort's name is a fraud.
5. **TikTok Drama Center** needs a registered business (TikTok's own guide, step 3), so it is not on this list.

## What to put in your pitch

- [ ] **A one-line story:** who, what goes wrong, what the twist is.
- [ ] **Your portfolio:** a link to your Instagram or TikTok channel, or wherever your dramas are.
- [ ] **Episodes × length:** how many episodes you have, and how long each one is.
- [ ] **Format:** vertical video (9:16), the language, subtitles or not.
- [ ] **Two or three finished sample episodes** (a private link).
- [ ] **Proof you own the rights:** the story, the characters, the music, and which AI tools you used (and that their terms let you sell the result).

## A short e-mail you can copy (our template)

```
Subject: AI drama pitch: [TITLE] ([N] episodes × [LENGTH])

Hi [APP] team,

I make vertical AI dramas. [TITLE] is about [ONE-LINE STORY].

- Episodes: [N] × [LENGTH], vertical 9:16, [LANGUAGE]
- Sample episodes: [PRIVATE LINK]
- My channel: [INSTAGRAM / TIKTOK LINK]
- Rights: I own the story, the characters and the music. Made with [TOOLS].

Could you tell me what you need from a content provider to consider it?

Thanks,
[NAME]
```
