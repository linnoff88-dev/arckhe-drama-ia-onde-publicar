# AI Drama Platform Map: where to publish and who pays (round 2, checked 19 Nov 2026)

From the AndyNoCode video about where to publish AI dramas and who pays. Round 2 = we went back to each company's own page (19 Nov 2026) and rebuilt the map on 20 Nov 2026.

**26 places where your AI drama can earn money**, in the three doors from the video. Every entry has its source and the page's own date. Amounts are in US dollars (1 US$ = 6.67 CNY, 17 Nov 2026).

**Read this first**
- "Up to" is the most you can get, not what most people get.
- Programs change, and they run in rounds that end. Open the source link and read the rules on the day you apply.
- Many programs are not open in every country. When a page lists no countries, we say so.
- "No date" means the page shows no date. "Our capture" means we kept a full-page screenshot on that day.
- News-only numbers say "(news)". We could not find them on the company's own page.

## The three doors

1. **Door 1, the big drama apps:** no upload button. You pitch them (`2-pitch-checklist.md`).
2. **Door 2, the open platforms:** anyone can upload, but each has a bar to clear. The AI tools that pay you to post sit here too.
3. **Door 3, new apps that want creators:** the easiest way in right now, but the newest rules.

## Door 1: the big drama apps (you pitch, you don't upload)

There is no upload button. They make their own dramas or buy them, mostly from studios. One person can only pitch, by e-mail or a contact form. See `2-pitch-checklist.md` for what to send.

### 1. ReelShort (studio: Crazy Maple) · Pitch only (studio form)
- **What it pays:** There is no upload or submit button: the ReelShort footer only has "Contact Us". Its studio, Crazy Maple, has a contact form with the option "Story Ideas and Submissions".
- **The catch:** To send that form you must tick "I agree with the Content Submission Agreement", but the page doesn't show the agreement. So send a short pitch, not your whole script. The studio also warns: "Any platform or individual claiming to offer investment returns using the ReelShort name is engaging in fraud."
- **Source:** [reelshort.com](https://www.reelshort.com/), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [Crazy Maple contact form](https://www.crazymaplestudio.com/contact), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [Crazy Maple statement](https://www.crazymaplestudio.com/statement), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026)

### 2. GoodShort · Pitch by e-mail
- **What it pays:** Its business page: if you are a content provider and "would like to sell your ebooks/audiobooks/reels through GoodShort's channels, please email us at copyright@goodnovel.com". Business contact: contact@goodshort.com. There is no upload form.
- **The catch:** No public terms (share, length, rights). Its script competition (21 Sep–10 Oct 2026, up to $10,000, horizontal AI series) is closed; we saw it only in search results.
- **Source:** [goodshort.com/business](https://www.goodshort.com/business), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026)

### 3. DramaBox · No public way to apply
- **What it pays:** News reports say its owner (Dianzhong) offers up to $50,000 for a top-grade finished AI drama outside China, then a 50:50 share, and $2,000–20,000 plus a 10–20% share for scripts. An industry account (DataEye) says the $50,000 is a reward for the top grade ("S+") only. No DramaBox or Dianzhong page shows these numbers.
- **The catch:** The only public contact is an e-mail address on its support page (dramaboxapp@gmail.com). No form, no rules for who can apply. dramaboxdb.com blocked our browser.
- **Source:** [DramaBox support page](https://support.dramaboxdb.com/), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [Securities Daily via 10jqka (Chinese)](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260417/c676078399.shtml), 17 Apr 2026 · [DataEye on The Paper (Chinese)](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33060782), 27 Apr 2026

### 4. PineDrama (TikTok's drama app) · Studios only
- **What it pays:** Studio deals only (for example Made By Us, Oct 2026). Launched in the US and Brazil in Jan 2026.
- **The catch:** We found no open submission page.
- **Source:** [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/10/14/tiktok-says-yes-to-more-creator-led-microdramas-from-made-by-us-and-hoorae/), 14 Oct 2026

### 5. Hongguo (紅果, China) · China only
- **What it pays:** From 13 Nov 2026, S-grade AI-drama scripts get ¥15,000 (≈ $2,250) guaranteed plus a 20% share, paid in two parts (25% / 75%), for scripts of 70+ episodes. This is for scripts, not finished dramas.
- **The catch:** China only. The rules are inside a login-only creator center (shortdramas.com); we have one news report. The script's copyright goes to the platform.
- **Source:** [IT Times via Sohu (Chinese)](https://www.sohu.com/a/1101926147_121423427), 15 Nov 2026 · [shortdramas.com (creator center)](https://www.shortdramas.com/), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026)

**What the makers get from a drama app:** it depends on the deal. Offers we found: a 20% share for Hongguo scripts (news), 50% for DramaBox's top finished dramas (news), and 70% on NEXTALE (its own page, door 3). One report says makers often get just 10–15% of the viewer money; we found only that one source. Sources: [TMTPost (Chinese)](https://www.tmtpost.com/8189319.html), 10 Nov 2026, and the rows above.

## Door 2: open platforms (anyone can upload)

Anyone can upload, but each one has a bar to clear: countries, followers, watch hours, or a registered company.

### 6. YouTube Partner Program · Open in many countries
- **What it pays:** Ad share. Today you need 1,000 subscribers, plus 4,000 watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days). From 1 Feb 2027, new channels need 8,000 hours (365 days) or 20M Shorts views (90 days). Channels already in the program keep their status. Accept the new terms in YouTube Studio by 31 Jan 2027. Shorts: you keep 45% of your share. From 1 Feb 2027 you need 10M Shorts views (90 days) to earn from the Shorts Creator Pool each month.
- **The catch:** Mass-produced videos from the same template, including easy AI videos, can't earn. That rule is on YouTube's policy page (in place since 15 Jul 2025); YouTube made it clearer in July 2026. AI itself is allowed. Realistic AI must get the AI label.
- **Source:** [YouTube Help: YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en), no date · [YouTube Help: changes from 1 Feb 2027](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=en), no date · [YouTube Help: Shorts pay](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en), no date · [YouTube Help: monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en), rule dated 15 Jul 2025 · [YouTube Help: AI label](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en), no date · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/), 20 Jul 2026

### 7. TikTok Creator Rewards Program · Open in 8 countries
- **What it pays:** Rewards for original videos over 1 minute. Each video needs at least 1,000 For You views. No Duets, Stitches or Photo Mode. Ads and paid promotions don't count.
- **The catch:** Only in the US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Mexico and Brazil. 18 or older. 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days.
- **Source:** [TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program), updated 10 Sep 2026 (our capture)

### 8. TikTok Drama Center · Registered businesses only
- **What it pays:** TikTok's place for short-drama partners: ad revenue share and paid unlocks, plus translation into other languages. Its page says premium content, "including … AI-generated adaptations", is "eligible for higher revenue incentives".
- **The catch:** TikTok's own step-by-step guide: step 3 is "Verify your business entity", and that is "a mandatory step for signing agreements, uploading content, and receiving payments". So it is not a door for one person without a company. No rates on its pages.
- **Source:** [tiktokdramacenter.com](https://www.tiktokdramacenter.com/), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [TikTok for Business: How to publish TikTok Micro-series](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-publish-your-mini-drama-content), no date (our captures, 19–20 Nov 2026) · [The Paper (Chinese), translation offer](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33341856), 10 Jun 2026

### 9. Instagram · No ad share
- **What it pays:** Instagram's own earn page lists three ways to earn: bonuses ("for the performance of your reels, carousels and single image posts"), gifts and subscriptions. There is no ad-revenue share.
- **The catch:** The page shows no amounts and no country list. Brand deals need the "Paid partnership" label. The creator in the video earned from the AI tool's program, not from Instagram.
- **Source:** [Instagram for Creators: earn](https://creators.instagram.com/earn-cash-making-what-you-love?locale=en_US), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026)

### 10. Facebook (Content Monetization + Creator Fast Track) · Invite / apply
- **What it pays:** Content Monetization pays for Reels, Stories, photos and text posts (repeat views and views under 5 seconds don't count). Creator Fast Track (from Mar 2026) gives 3 months of guaranteed pay to creators who are already big elsewhere: $1,000 a month with 100,000+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, $3,000 a month with 1M+, and $100–450 a month with 20,000+.
- **The catch:** Content Monetization is invite only, but anyone can fill in the interest form (Professional Dashboard → Monetization). Reels must be published in certain countries and languages; Meta's list did not open for us.
- **Source:** [Facebook creators blog](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/new-tips-to-help-you-earn-on-facebook-content-monetization/?locale=en_US), 30 Mar 2026 · [Meta newsroom: Creator Fast Track](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/), 18 Mar 2026 · [Meta Business Help: countries and languages](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/267128784014981?locale=en_US), no date

## Door 2, part 2: AI tools that pay you to post there

You post on the open platforms, and the AI tool pays you, because your video is an ad for the tool. Usually you must make it with their tool, credit them and post where they say. The amounts are "up to", and the programs run in rounds that end.

### 11. OiiOii Creator Promotion Program 2.0 · Round open until 30 Nov 2026
- **What it pays:** Pays by likes on one video. X, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts: 500 / 3,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 likes = $65 / $315 / $1,265 / $6,330. YouTube videos over 2 minutes: 500 / 3,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 likes = $125 / $635 / $1,900 / $12,665. Videos that miss these tiers can win credits in a monthly pick. Only 224 creators had joined by 19 Nov.
- **The catch:** How it works: apply with their form (they reply within 7 days), make the video with OiiOii, post it, then send them the link. They check likes on the 10th, 20th and 30th; you sign their "Creator Collaboration Agreement" online, and they pay once a month. Rules: the first line of your caption and a pinned comment must say "This video was made with oiioii.ai"; tag their account; keep a visible OiiOii watermark; videos over 1 minute (over 2 minutes on YouTube). At most 3 payouts a month; only your best platform counts; only the highest tier is paid. Bought likes or fake traffic = out. They can use your video in their ads. "Program terms are subject to change." This round runs 1 Oct–30 Nov 2026; the rules changed on 17 Nov for new sign-ups. Watch for the next round. OiiOii also runs short contests (OiiOii × Kling 4.0, 9–22 Nov 2026, win $2,000).
- **Source:** [OiiOii campaign page (needs a login; read in our team's own account)](https://www.oiioii.ai/campaigns/dfa98587-feb3-4136-95fe-00e2e0e3c2d8), rules update 17 Nov 2026; read 19 Nov 2026 · [OiiOii on X: "Earn up to $12000+"](https://x.com/OiiOii_AI/status/2105462710578139524), 30 Sep 2026 · [OiiOii on X: Kling 4.0 contest](https://x.com/i/status/2119807221991178351), 9 Nov 2026

### 12. Higgsfield Earn · Running, some countries
- **What it pays:** Rewards "based on your views and performance": pay per approved video, plus 24-hour and day-7 bonuses. Max $2,500 per video (lifetime) and max $1,000 on the first day. Paid through Garna. Its CEO said it paid "over $800,000 to more than 10,000 creators" in 3 weeks (Feb 2026): about $80 each on average.
- **The catch:** Only in some countries: its terms say Higgsfield "maintains an Allow List of eligible countries", and the list is not public. "Payout rates are confidential." Post to Instagram (the help center also says YouTube or X). We found no creator posting their own payout amount, and a few public complaints about the payout service (Trustpilot).
- **Source:** [higgsfield.ai/earn](https://higgsfield.ai/earn), no date (our capture) · [Higgsfield help center](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/business/how-do-i-join-a-partnership-or-creator-program), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [Higgsfield Earn terms](https://creators.higgsfield.ai/terms), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [Higgsfield CEO on X](https://x.com/i/status/2021356329416393164), 10 Feb 2026 · [Garna on Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/garna.io), read 19 Nov 2026

## Door 2, part 3: affiliate links on those platforms

You earn a commission when someone buys a product you tagged.

### 24. YouTube Shopping affiliate · By invitation, 18 countries
- **What it pays:** Tag products in your videos and earn a commission on each sale. The brand or store sets the commission.
- **The catch:** YouTube invites you: "To be invited to the program, you must meet these minimum creator eligibility requirements". You must be in YouTube's Partner Program. Only in Argentina, Brazil, Germany, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, Philippines, Poland, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, the UK, the US and Vietnam. Shopping opens from 500 subscribers (500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days; no change in 2027). Not for music (Official Artist) channels or Made for Kids channels.
- **Source:** [YouTube Help: Shopping affiliate](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en), no date (our capture) · [YouTube Help: 500-subscriber level](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en), no date

### 25. Amazon Associates (US) · Open (US)
- **What it pays:** Up to 10%. Most categories pay 3–4.5% ("all other categories" 4%).
- **The catch:** Made for websites and apps. Social creators are sent to the Amazon Influencer Program, whose rules and countries are not public.
- **Source:** [Amazon Associates rates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ), no date

## Door 3: new apps that want creators

New apps need dramas, so they make it easy to get in. Their rules are new and can change, so keep a copy of everything.

### 26. NEXTALE · Open until 14 Dec 2026
- **What it pays:** NEXTALE Award: finish a series of 20+ episodes by 14 Dec and keep 100% of the money from its paid views for the first 6 months after launch (win or not), plus 100% of tips. Prizes: $2,000 for one series, and $100 each for the first 10 complete series. Winners on 20 Dec. Their normal creator share after that: 70% of episode unlocks, 70% of subscriptions (by watch time), 100% of tips.
- **The catch:** Three steps and no application form: make a free Studio account, upload 20+ episodes in NEXTALE Studio before 14 Dec, and pass "a quick check that the episodes are complete". Any country, any language, any AI tool; you keep your IP. The catches: the app was not live yet when we checked (it says it launches in December); the creator agreement is only inside NEXTALE Studio (behind a login); and the company is new: Nextale FZE, registered in Sharjah, UAE (terms updated 23 Oct 2026). Keep a copy of everything you upload.
- **Source:** [NEXTALE Award](https://nextale.com/nextale-award), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [NEXTALE for creators](https://nextale.com/creators), no date (our capture, 19 Nov 2026) · [NEXTALE terms](https://nextale.com/terms), last updated 23 Oct 2026

## Product placement and brand dramas (more ways to earn)

- **An AI character's ad price, and what really happened:** Fang Taozi, the AI heroine of a Chinese AI drama, had 412,000 Douyin fans (Aug 2026). Her listed ad prices went up to ¥258,000 (≈ $38,700) for a video over 60 s. But her team says the price was set high on purpose, and her first known deal was ¥68,288 (≈ $10,240). They never got that money: in that ad, for colored contact lenses, the AI character said she wore them all day. The video was taken down, and on 13 Nov 2026 the market regulator fined them ¥68,288, the same as the ad fee. Sources: [STAR Market Daily, cls.cn (Chinese)](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2445817), 5 Aug 2026 · [Phoenix News, ifeng (Chinese)](https://original.ifeng.com/c/8y6lkL4r5OE), 16 Nov 2026 · [Beijing Daily (Chinese)](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2026/11/16/12032524.shtml), 16 Nov 2026.
- **A brand AI drama:** KFC Arabia made 3 AI episodes: 4M+ views, 85,000 likes and 130,000 shares on Meta and TikTok, without paid promotion (numbers from the brand). Source: [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/kfc-arabia-turns-cheddar-sauce-shortage-into-viral-ai-led-campaign/), 20 May 2026.
- **China price:** a brand-custom AI drama of about 20 minutes can reach ¥200,000–300,000 (≈ $30,000–45,000) (news). Source: [21st Century Business Herald, citing People's Daily (Chinese)](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260912/herald/56964b91baeb2372ba0df9640eee35d8.html), 12 Sep 2026.

**Three rules for product placement**
1. Put the product inside the story.
2. Never let an AI character say it used the product.
3. Turn on the paid promotion label when you upload: YouTube "Yes, my video includes branded content"; TikTok "commercial content disclosure"; Instagram "Paid partnership". Sources: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en) · [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-turn-on-the-commercial-content-disclosure-setting-in-tiktok), updated Jan 2026 · Instagram Help (search result only).
